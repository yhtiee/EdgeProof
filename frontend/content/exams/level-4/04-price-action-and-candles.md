::question{id="pa-1" lesson="l4-anatomy-of-a-candle"}
A candle's body shows:
- [x] The range between its open and its close
- [ ] The highest and lowest prices
- [ ] The trading volume
? The body runs from open to close; the wicks show the high and low.
::
::question{id="pa-2" lesson="l4-anatomy-of-a-candle"}
A candle opens at 1.0850 and closes at 1.0870. It is:
- [x] A rising (bullish) candle with a 20-pip body
- [ ] A falling candle with a 20-pip body
- [ ] A rising candle with a 70-pip body
? Close above open means a rising candle. 1.0870 − 1.0850 = 20 pips.
::
::question{id="pa-3" lesson="l4-anatomy-of-a-candle"}
A long lower wick means:
- [x] Price fell during the period, then buyers pushed it back up
- [ ] Price only rose during the period
- [ ] The market was closed
? The wick shows a low that didn't hold by the close.
::
::question{id="pa-4" lesson="l4-anatomy-of-a-candle"}
A small body with long wicks on both sides suggests:
- [x] A tug-of-war with no clear winner
- [ ] A strong uptrend
- [ ] A strong downtrend
? Price moved both ways but finished near where it started.
::
::question{id="pa-5" lesson="l4-candle-patterns-in-context"}
Why does the context around a candle pattern matter?
- [x] The same pattern means different things at a key level, mid-range or against the trend
- [ ] Patterns work the same everywhere
- [ ] Context only matters on weekly charts
? A pattern at a tested level in line with the trend differs from one in the middle of nowhere.
::
::question{id="pa-6" lesson="l4-candle-patterns-in-context"}
Research on candle patterns on their own (without other rules) generally finds:
- [x] Weak or inconsistent edges once costs are included
- [ ] They predict the next move most of the time
- [ ] They are guaranteed to work on major pairs
? Patterns need context and testing; alone they rarely add much.
::
::question{id="pa-7" lesson="l4-candle-patterns-in-context"}
A bullish pattern forms just under strong resistance in a downtrend. What's sensible?
- [x] Treat it with caution; the context works against it
- [ ] Buy a big position, since the pattern is bullish
- [ ] Ignore resistance and trends
? Context can outweigh a single pattern.
::
::question{id="pa-8" lesson="l4-candle-patterns-in-context"}
How do you find out whether a candle pattern helps your strategy?
- [x] Define it precisely and test it over many examples, including costs
- [ ] Look at the three best examples you can find
- [ ] Ask whether it looks convincing
? Cherry-picked examples always look good. Only a full test tells you.
::
