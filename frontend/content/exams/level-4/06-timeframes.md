::question{id="tf-1" lesson="l4-choosing-a-timeframe"}
Which factor should most shape your choice of timeframe?
- [x] How much time you have to watch the market, and your costs per trade
- [ ] Which timeframe gives the most trades
- [ ] Whatever timeframe is popular online
? Your schedule and costs decide which timeframe you can trade well.
::
::question{id="tf-2" lesson="l4-choosing-a-timeframe"}
Compared with a daily chart, a 5-minute chart usually means:
- [x] Smaller moves per trade, so spreads and commissions take a bigger share
- [ ] Lower costs as a share of each trade
- [ ] No losing trades
? Smaller targets make fixed costs weigh more.
::
::question{id="tf-3" lesson="l4-choosing-a-timeframe"}
You have a full-time job and can check charts twice a day. Which timeframe fits best?
- [ ] 1-minute
- [x] 4-hour or daily
- [ ] 15-second
? Higher timeframes suit people who can't watch the screen all day.
::
::question{id="tf-4" lesson="l4-choosing-a-timeframe"}
Does a lower timeframe give you more control or less risk?
- [ ] Yes, it's safer
- [x] No; it gives more trades, more noise and higher cost per unit of movement
- [ ] Only on major pairs
? More trades don't mean better ones.
::
::question{id="tf-5" lesson="l4-noise-and-the-bigger-picture"}
"Noise" on a chart means:
- [x] Small random price wiggles that don't reflect a meaningful move
- [ ] The sound alerts on your platform
- [ ] News headlines
? Lower timeframes carry more noise relative to the real move.
::
::question{id="tf-6" lesson="l4-noise-and-the-bigger-picture"}
A 15-minute chart looks bearish, but the daily chart is in a clear uptrend. What should you keep in mind?
- [x] The short-term dip may just be noise inside the bigger trend
- [ ] The daily chart no longer matters
- [ ] The trend has definitely reversed
? Higher timeframes give context to what you see on lower ones.
::
::question{id="tf-7" lesson="l4-noise-and-the-bigger-picture"}
Why check a higher timeframe before trading a lower one?
- [x] To see the bigger trend and key levels the lower chart might hide
- [ ] Because the broker requires it
- [ ] To find more trades
? The bigger picture helps you avoid trading straight into major levels.
::
::question{id="tf-8" lesson="l4-noise-and-the-bigger-picture"}
Switching timeframes until you find one that "agrees" with the trade you want is:
- [ ] Good analysis
- [x] Confirmation bias
- [ ] Required for every trade
? Decide which timeframes you use in your rules beforehand, then stick to them.
::
