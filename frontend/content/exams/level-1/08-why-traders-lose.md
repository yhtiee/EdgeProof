::question{id="wtl-1" lesson="l1-the-evidence-on-retail-traders"}
European regulators found that, on typical CFD platforms, retail accounts that lose money make up about:
- [ ] 10–20%
- [ ] 40–50%
- [x] 74–89%
? ESMA reported that 74–89% of retail CFD accounts typically lose money.
::
::question{id="wtl-2" lesson="l1-the-evidence-on-retail-traders"}
Why do regulated brokers in many countries show a "% of accounts lose money" warning?
- [x] Regulators require it so clients see the real odds before trading
- [ ] It is a marketing trick to look honest
- [ ] It only applies to professional traders
? The warning is a legal requirement based on each broker's own client results.
::
::question{id="wtl-3" lesson="l1-the-evidence-on-retail-traders"}
What does the evidence on retail traders suggest you should do?
- [ ] Trade bigger to beat the odds faster
- [x] Expect to lose while learning, and prove an edge on small risk before scaling up
- [ ] Ignore it; it doesn't apply to smart people
? Most people lose. Treat early trading as paid education with tiny risk.
::
::question{id="wtl-4" lesson="l1-why-most-traders-lose"}
Which combination drives most retail losses?
- [x] Too much leverage, costs, no proven edge and emotional decisions
- [ ] Too few indicators on the chart
- [ ] Trading only major pairs
? Losses come from oversizing, costs and the lack of a tested edge, made worse by emotion.
::
::question{id="wtl-5" lesson="l1-why-most-traders-lose"}
Why is high leverage dangerous for a beginner?
- [ ] It makes the spread wider
- [x] A small move against a large position can wipe out the account quickly
- [ ] It stops you placing stop-losses
? Large positions turn ordinary moves into account-ending losses.
::
::question{id="wtl-6" lesson="l1-why-most-traders-lose"}
A trader without a tested edge who trades often will usually:
- [ ] Break even over time
- [x] Lose slowly, roughly by the amount of their costs
- [ ] Make money, because markets go up
? With no edge, results are random before costs, and costs push them below zero.
::
::question{id="wtl-7" lesson="l1-why-most-traders-lose"}
Which behaviour is a common cause of large losses?
- [x] Removing a stop-loss to "give the trade room"
- [ ] Keeping a trading journal
- [ ] Risking a small fixed percentage per trade
? Moving or removing stops turns small, planned losses into large, unplanned ones.
::
::question{id="wtl-8" lesson="l1-the-evidence-on-retail-traders"}
A broker's warning says 76% of its retail accounts lose money. What does that tell you?
- [ ] You have a 76% chance of profit
- [x] Roughly three in four of its clients lost money over the period measured
- [ ] The broker loses money on 76% of trades
? It describes client outcomes at that broker. It is a base rate to respect, not a prediction for any single person.
::
