::question{id="ir-1" lesson="l5-why-interest-rates-move-currencies"}
Why do higher interest rates tend to support a currency?
- [x] Savers and investors earn more by holding it, so demand for it rises
- [ ] Higher rates print more of the currency
- [ ] Higher rates make exports cheaper
? Money tends to flow toward higher returns, other things being equal.
::
::question{id="ir-2" lesson="l5-why-interest-rates-move-currencies"}
Markets fully expected a rate rise, and it happened. What usually follows?
- [x] Little reaction, because the rise was already priced in
- [ ] A huge rally, every time
- [ ] The currency always falls
? Prices move mostly on surprises and changing expectations.
::
::question{id="ir-3" lesson="l5-why-interest-rates-move-currencies"}
What moves currencies most around interest rates?
- [ ] The current rate level alone
- [x] Changes in what markets expect rates to do next
- [ ] The day of the week the decision is made
? A shift in the expected path of rates carries the most new information.
::
::question{id="ir-4" lesson="l5-why-interest-rates-move-currencies"}
Country A's rates are expected to rise while Country B's are expected to stay flat. Other things equal, A's currency against B's will likely:
- [x] Strengthen
- [ ] Weaken
- [ ] Stay exactly the same
? The widening expected rate gap tends to favour Country A's currency.
::
::question{id="ir-5" lesson="l5-the-carry-trade"}
A carry trade is:
- [x] Buying a high-interest currency against a low-interest one to earn the rate gap
- [ ] Carrying cash abroad on holiday
- [ ] Holding trades only during the day
? The trader earns the interest difference through positive swaps.
::
::question{id="ir-6" type="numeric" answer="28" tolerance="0" unit="$" lesson="l5-the-carry-trade"}
Your carry trade earns $4 per night on 1 lot. You hold it through all five weekday rollovers (Monday to Friday), and Wednesday's rollover counts three times. How much swap do you earn, in dollars?
? Monday 1 + Tuesday 1 + Wednesday 3 + Thursday 1 + Friday 1 = 7 nights. 7 × $4 = $28.
::
::question{id="ir-7" lesson="l5-the-carry-trade"}
What is the main risk of a carry trade?
- [ ] The swap is too small to matter
- [x] When markets get nervous, many carry traders exit at once and the price can fall fast, wiping out months of interest
- [ ] Brokers ban carry trades
? Carry trades can unwind sharply in risk-off moments.
::
::question{id="ir-8" lesson="l5-the-carry-trade"}
Positive swap on a trade means:
- [x] You're paid interest for holding the position overnight
- [ ] You pay a fee every night
- [ ] Your trade is guaranteed to win
? You receive swap when you hold the higher-yielding currency against a lower-yielding one, after the broker's markup.
::
