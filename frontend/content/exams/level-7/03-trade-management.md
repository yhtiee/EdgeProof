::question{id="tm-1" lesson="l7-breakeven-partials-and-trailing-stops"}
Moving your stop to breakeven means:
- [x] Moving the stop to your entry price once the trade has moved in your favour
- [ ] Closing the trade at once
- [ ] Doubling the position
? If price then comes back to entry, the trade closes at roughly zero.
::
::question{id="tm-2" lesson="l7-breakeven-partials-and-trailing-stops"}
What's the downside of moving to breakeven early?
- [x] Some trades that would have gone on to win are stopped out at zero
- [ ] It increases your risk
- [ ] There's no downside
? Breakeven trades losses for missed wins; the net effect has to be tested.
::
::question{id="tm-3" lesson="l7-breakeven-partials-and-trailing-stops"}
Taking partial profits at a first target usually:
- [x] Raises the win rate but cuts the size of the biggest wins
- [ ] Raises both win rate and average win
- [ ] Has no effect on results
? Banking part early reduces what the best trades can deliver.
::
::question{id="tm-4" lesson="l7-breakeven-partials-and-trailing-stops"}
A trailing stop:
- [x] Moves behind price as it moves your way, locking in part of the gain
- [ ] Moves away from price to give the trade room
- [ ] Is fixed at entry and never changes
? It follows price in your favour only.
::
::question{id="tm-5" lesson="l7-testing-management-rules"}
A trader adds a breakeven rule and their win rate rises from 40% to 55%. What should they check next?
- [x] Whether expectancy went up, down or stayed the same
- [ ] Nothing; a higher win rate is always better
- [ ] Whether their friends like the new rule
? Judge management by its effect on expectancy, not win rate or comfort.
::
::question{id="tm-6" lesson="l7-testing-management-rules"}
Why test management rules on the same trades with and without the rule?
- [x] So the only difference is the rule, and its effect can be measured
- [ ] To make the test longer
- [ ] To find the best-looking result
? Holding everything else fixed isolates the rule's effect.
::
::question{id="tm-7" lesson="l7-testing-management-rules"}
Managing trades "by feel" (closing when nervous, holding when hopeful) usually:
- [x] Adds untested noise and often lowers expectancy
- [ ] Improves every strategy
- [ ] Has no effect
? Unplanned management is a hidden, untested change to the strategy.
::
::question{id="tm-8" lesson="l7-testing-management-rules"}
Breakeven, partials and trailing stops each:
- [x] Trade something away, so each needs testing
- [ ] Are free improvements
- [ ] Always make results worse
? Each changes the balance between win rate and payoff.
::
