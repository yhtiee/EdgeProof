::question{id="pf-1" lesson="l7-how-prop-challenges-work"}
A prop firm challenge typically requires you to:
- [x] Reach a profit target without breaking daily loss and maximum drawdown limits
- [ ] Trade only one pair
- [ ] Win every trade
? Passing means hitting the target while staying inside the risk rules.
::
::question{id="pf-2" lesson="l7-how-prop-challenges-work"}
A challenge has a 5% daily loss limit. You risk 1% per trade. How many full losses in one day would breach it?
- [x] More than five
- [ ] Two
- [ ] Ten would still be fine
? Five full losses reach 5%; any more breaks the limit.
::
::question{id="pf-3" lesson="l7-how-prop-challenges-work"}
Some firms measure maximum drawdown from the highest balance reached. That means:
- [x] The limit moves up as your account grows, which can be tighter than it looks
- [ ] Your limit gets looser over time
- [ ] Drawdown is not counted
? A trailing drawdown rule follows your peak.
::
::question{id="pf-4" lesson="l7-how-prop-challenges-work"}
Which is a common prop firm rule besides the profit target and loss limits?
- [x] Bans on trading around news, or limits on holding over weekends
- [ ] A guaranteed salary
- [ ] Unlimited leverage
? Rules vary; read every one before you pay.
::
::question{id="pf-5" lesson="l7-prop-firm-risks"}
Why can a good strategy still fail a prop challenge?
- [x] Normal losing streaks can hit the tight drawdown limits before the edge plays out
- [ ] Good strategies never fail
- [ ] Prop firms only accept losing strategies
? Tight limits leave little room for ordinary drawdowns.
::
::question{id="pf-6" lesson="l7-prop-firm-risks"}
What happens to challenge fees if you keep failing and retrying?
- [x] They add up, and can cost more than the funded account is likely to pay out
- [ ] They are refunded automatically
- [ ] They get cheaper every time
? Repeated fees are a real cost to weigh against expected payouts.
::
::question{id="pf-7" lesson="l7-prop-firm-risks"}
Which is a risk with the prop firm itself?
- [x] Firms have closed, changed their rules, or refused payouts
- [ ] Firms are guaranteed by the government
- [ ] Firms must pay any profit you make
? The firm is a counterparty risk, like a broker.
::
::question{id="pf-8" lesson="l7-prop-firm-risks"}
Before paying for a challenge, what should you check?
- [x] How often your strategy's drawdowns would breach the limits, using your own risk and win rate
- [ ] Only the profit target
- [ ] Whether other people passed
? Simulate your own strategy against the rules before you pay.
::
