::question{id="fg-1" lesson="l3-why-losses-hurt-more"}
Research by Kahneman and Tversky found that people:
- [x] Feel losses more strongly than equal-sized gains
- [ ] Feel gains and losses equally
- [ ] Enjoy losses more than gains
? This is called loss aversion. A $100 loss stings more than a $100 win pleases.
::
::question{id="fg-2" lesson="l3-why-losses-hurt-more"}
Loss aversion often leads traders to:
- [x] Hold losing trades too long and cut winners too early
- [ ] Hold winners too long and cut losers too early
- [ ] Trade exactly to plan
? Closing a loser makes the pain real, so people hope; closing a winner locks in the good feeling.
::
::question{id="fg-3" lesson="l3-why-losses-hurt-more"}
Which rule best protects you from holding losers out of hope?
- [ ] Decide later when the trade looks bad
- [x] Set your stop before entry and never move it further away
- [ ] Average down when the trade goes against you
? A stop decided in advance takes the emotion out of the exit.
::
::question{id="fg-4" lesson="l3-why-losses-hurt-more"}
You close a winning trade early because you "don't want to give it back". If you do this often, what happens?
- [ ] Your expectancy rises
- [x] Your average win shrinks, which can turn a winning strategy into a losing one
- [ ] Nothing changes
? Cutting winners short lowers the payoff the strategy depends on.
::
::question{id="fg-5" lesson="l3-chasing-and-fear-of-missing-out"}
Fear of missing out (FOMO) usually shows up as:
- [x] Jumping into a move that has already run, without your setup
- [ ] Waiting patiently for your setup
- [ ] Skipping trades you have no plan for
? FOMO pulls you into late, unplanned entries with poor risk.
::
::question{id="fg-6" lesson="l3-chasing-and-fear-of-missing-out"}
Why is chasing a big move risky?
- [ ] Brokers charge more for late entries
- [x] Entries far from a logical stop mean a bigger risk, or a stop in a poor place
- [ ] Big moves never continue
? Late entries usually mean worse prices and weaker risk-to-reward.
::
::question{id="fg-7" lesson="l3-chasing-and-fear-of-missing-out"}
You missed a trade that went on to make 5R. What's the healthiest response?
- [ ] Enter now at any price to catch the rest
- [x] Let it go; another setup will come, and missed trades cost nothing
- [ ] Double your size on the next setup to make up for it
? There will always be another trade. Chasing turns a missed profit into a real loss.
::
::question{id="fg-8" lesson="l3-chasing-and-fear-of-missing-out"}
Greed after a winning streak often shows up as:
- [x] Raising size or loosening rules because you feel unbeatable
- [ ] Taking a break
- [ ] Reviewing your journal
? Winning streaks breed overconfidence. Keep size fixed by the plan.
::
