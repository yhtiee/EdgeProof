::question{id="rt-1" lesson="l3-what-revenge-trading-is"}
Revenge trading is:
- [x] Taking unplanned trades to win back a loss quickly
- [ ] Trading against a broker you dislike
- [ ] Following your plan after a loss
? It is driven by the urge to erase a loss, not by a setup.
::
::question{id="rt-2" lesson="l3-what-revenge-trading-is"}
Which is a sign you're revenge trading?
- [ ] You enter a planned setup at your normal size
- [x] You enter straight after a loss, bigger than usual, without your setup
- [ ] You stop trading for the day after hitting your limit
? Bigger size, faster entries and no setup after a loss are classic signs.
::
::question{id="rt-3" lesson="l3-what-revenge-trading-is"}
Why does revenge trading make things worse?
- [x] It adds more risk at the moment your judgement is worst
- [ ] It always wins, so it's addictive
- [ ] Brokers block accounts that do it
? Emotional, oversized trades turn a small loss into a big one.
::
::question{id="rt-4" lesson="l3-what-revenge-trading-is"}
Revenge trading often starts because:
- [x] A loss hurts and a quick win would make the pain go away
- [ ] The market is very quiet
- [ ] The trader has a written plan
? Loss aversion drives the urge to "get it back" right away.
::
::question{id="rt-5" lesson="l3-breaking-the-revenge-loop"}
Your daily loss limit is 3R. You've lost three trades at −1R each. What should you do?
- [ ] Take one more trade to finish the day positive
- [x] Stop trading for the day
- [ ] Double the size so one win recovers it all
? The limit only works if you stop when you hit it.
::
::question{id="rt-6" lesson="l3-breaking-the-revenge-loop"}
A "cooling-off gap" rule might say:
- [x] No new trade within 30 minutes of a loss
- [ ] Trade immediately after a loss while you're focused
- [ ] Only trade after three losses in a row
? A short pause lets the urge to win it back fade.
::
::question{id="rt-7" lesson="l3-breaking-the-revenge-loop"}
Which rule helps prevent revenge trading?
- [ ] Increase size after each loss
- [x] Keep position size fixed by the plan, especially after a loss
- [ ] Remove the daily loss limit on bad days
? Fixed size stops one bad day becoming a disaster.
::
::question{id="rt-8" lesson="l3-breaking-the-revenge-loop"}
Why set loss limits before the session, not during it?
- [ ] Brokers require it
- [x] Decisions made while calm are better than ones made mid-loss
- [ ] Limits set later are illegal
? Pre-commitment protects you from your own state in the moment.
::
