::question{id="ro-1" lesson="l3-a-pre-session-routine"}
What is the main purpose of a pre-session routine?
- [x] To check your plan, the news and your own state before you trade
- [ ] To find as many trades as possible
- [ ] To guess the day's direction
? A short checklist sets you up to trade your plan, not your mood.
::
::question{id="ro-2" lesson="l3-a-pre-session-routine"}
Which item belongs on a pre-session checklist?
- [x] Big news releases today and the no-trade windows around them
- [ ] Yesterday's social media predictions
- [ ] Your account balance target for the week
? Knowing the news schedule avoids surprise volatility.
::
::question{id="ro-3" lesson="l3-a-pre-session-routine"}
During your routine you notice you slept badly and feel rushed. A good response is:
- [ ] Trade bigger to finish faster
- [x] Trade smaller, or skip the session
- [ ] Ignore it; feelings don't matter
? Your state affects decisions. Lower risk or step away when you're not at your best.
::
::question{id="ro-4" lesson="l3-a-pre-session-routine"}
Why set today's loss limit and maximum number of trades before the session?
- [ ] So you can ignore them later
- [x] So the decision is made while you're calm
- [ ] Because the broker requires it
? Limits fixed in advance protect you from in-the-moment emotion.
::
::question{id="ro-5" lesson="l3-daily-and-weekly-reviews"}
Which is the most important question in a daily review?
- [x] Did I follow my rules on every trade?
- [ ] How much money did I make?
- [ ] What will the market do tomorrow?
? Process comes first. Profit on one day says little; rule-following is in your control.
::
::question{id="ro-6" lesson="l3-daily-and-weekly-reviews"}
A weekly review should help you find:
- [x] Mistakes that repeat, and what they cost in R
- [ ] The perfect indicator
- [ ] A new strategy every week
? Repeated mistakes are the most valuable thing to fix.
::
::question{id="ro-7" lesson="l3-daily-and-weekly-reviews"}
How should a weekly review end?
- [ ] With a plan to double risk
- [x] With one specific thing to focus on next week
- [ ] With a list of every possible improvement
? One clear focus is easier to act on than a long list.
::
::question{id="ro-8" lesson="l3-daily-and-weekly-reviews"}
Brett Steenbarger describes routines and reviews as part of:
- [x] Self-coaching: small repeated habits that keep you trading your plan
- [ ] A way to predict prices
- [ ] Something only professionals need
? Regular routines turn good intentions into consistent behaviour.
::
