On paper, a 20% drawdown is just a number. In real life, it's weeks of losing, checking your account, and wondering whether your strategy has stopped working.

How you handle that feeling decides whether you keep following your rules, which decides whether your edge ever pays off.

---

Here's a strategy with a real edge: it wins 55% of the time, with wins and losses of the same size. Press "Flip again" several times and watch the dips along the way, even in the lines that end well.

::simulator{kind="coin-flip"}

---

Even with an edge, most lines spend long stretches below an earlier high. Those stretches are drawdowns, and they're normal.

The danger is what people do in them: they abandon a working strategy at the worst moment, or raise their risk to win it back faster.

::takeaway
Drawdowns are normal, even with an edge, and they feel worse than they look. Knowing what to expect in advance makes it easier to keep following your rules through them.
