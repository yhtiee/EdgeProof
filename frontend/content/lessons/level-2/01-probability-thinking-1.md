A probability is how often something happens over many tries. A fair coin lands heads with a probability of 50%. That doesn't mean every two flips give one head. It means that over thousands of flips, about half will be heads.

---

Probability tells you about the long run, not the next try. The next flip could be heads or tails. Nobody can know which.

That's uncomfortable, because people want to know what happens next. In trading, you never will.

---

::question{type="mc"}
A fair coin has landed heads five times in a row. What's the chance the next flip is heads?
- [ ] Less than 50%, because tails is due
- [x] 50%, the same as always
- [ ] More than 50%, because heads is on a streak
? The coin has no memory. Each flip is 50%, whatever happened before. Expecting tails to be "due" is called the gambler's fallacy.
::

---

Trading works the same way. A trade setup that wins 55% of the time can still lose five times in a row. Those losses don't make the next trade more likely to win, and they don't prove the setup has stopped working.

::takeaway
Probability describes what happens over many tries, never the next one. Past results don't make the next outcome more or less likely.
::
