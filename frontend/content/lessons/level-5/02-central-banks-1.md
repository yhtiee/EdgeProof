A [[central bank]] manages a country's money. Its main tool is the policy interest rate, which affects what banks, businesses and people pay to borrow.

Most central banks aim to keep [[inflation]] low and stable, and many also care about jobs and growth.

---

When inflation runs too high, central banks tend to raise rates to cool spending. When the economy weakens, they tend to cut rates to encourage it. Because rates affect currencies, every decision matters to forex traders.

---

They decide at scheduled meetings. The Federal Open Market Committee holds eight regularly scheduled meetings a year [@fed-fomc-faq]. From 2017 the Bank of England's Monetary Policy Committee meets eight times a year instead of monthly [@boe-mpc-schedule-2016].

---

Nigeria's central bank works the same way through its Monetary Policy Committee. At its November 2025 meeting, the CBN's Monetary Policy Committee held the Monetary Policy Rate at 27.0% [@cbn-mpc-november-2025].

Each central bank publishes its meeting dates in advance, and they appear on every economic calendar.

::takeaway
Central banks set interest rates to manage inflation and growth, at meetings scheduled in advance. Those meetings are among the biggest scheduled events for any currency.
