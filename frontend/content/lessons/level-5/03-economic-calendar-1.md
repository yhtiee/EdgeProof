An economic calendar lists scheduled releases for each currency: rate decisions, inflation, jobs, growth and more. Most show an impact level, often low, medium or high, to show which events tend to move prices most.

---

Some of the highest-impact events:

- **Central bank rate decisions**, which you met in the last module.
- **Inflation** data, such as the Consumer Price Index.
- **Jobs** data. The best known is the US jobs report with nonfarm payrolls, usually released on the first Friday of the month at 8:30 a.m. US Eastern time [@bls-employment-situation].
- **Growth** data, such as GDP.

---

Each entry usually shows three numbers:

- **Previous:** the last release.
- **Forecast** (or consensus): what economists expect.
- **Actual:** the real figure, filled in at release time.
