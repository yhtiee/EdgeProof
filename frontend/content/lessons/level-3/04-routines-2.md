The session isn't finished when you close your last trade. A few minutes of review is where most of the learning happens.

---

**A daily review can be three questions:**

1. Did I follow my rules on every trade?
2. If not, what pulled me off them?
3. What will I do differently tomorrow?

Notice what's missing: "Did I make money?" One day's result is mostly luck. Whether you followed your rules is not.

---

**A weekly review goes a little wider:**

1. How many trades followed every rule?
2. What did rule-breaking trades cost me in R?
3. Did any mistake repeat?
4. Did anything in my state (sleep, stress, time) affect my trading?
5. What's one thing to focus on next week?

EdgeProof's journal has both reviews built in.

Short daily and weekly reviews turn experience into learning. Review your process, not your profit: rule-following is what you control.
