In Level 2 you saw that single outcomes are random, even with an edge. Knowing that is easy. Feeling it while your money is on the line is hard.

Mark Douglas, a trading coach, summed up the mindset in five "fundamental truths" [@douglas-trading-in-the-zone]. Here they are in plain words.

---

1. **Anything can happen.** Any trade can lose, however good it looks.
2. **You don't need to know what happens next to make money.** You need an edge and the discipline to repeat it.
3. **Wins and losses arrive in random order.** Even a good strategy's next result is a coin toss weighted slightly in your favour.

---

4. **An edge is only a higher chance**, not a promise. It shows up over many trades, not on the next one.
5. **Every moment in the market is new.** A chart that looks like last week's won't necessarily end like last week's.

---

- [ ] Wins come in streaks, so this one will win too
- [x] Anything can happen: the next trade can lose, so risk the same planned amount
- [ ] An edge means the setup can't lose now
? Past wins don't change the next trade's odds. Keep the same risk and rules; don't size up because of a streak.
::

---

::takeaway
Any single trade can go either way, even with an edge. Accepting that is what lets you take every valid trade with the same planned risk, without fear or overconfidence.
::
