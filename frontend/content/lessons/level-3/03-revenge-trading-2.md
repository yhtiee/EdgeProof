Willpower in the moment is weak. The way to beat revenge trading is to decide the limits beforehand, while you're calm, and write them down.

---

**Limits that work well:**

- **A daily loss limit**, such as 2R or 3R. Hit it, and you're done for the day.
- **A losing-streak rule**, such as stopping after three losses in a row.
- **A cooling-off gap**, such as no new trade within 30 minutes of a loss.
- **Fixed size.** Never bigger than your plan, especially after a loss.

EdgeProof's rulebook has fields for each of these.

---

Your never-again list from onboarding helps too. It shows on every trade you log, as a reminder of what's hurt you before.

---

::question{type="numeric" answer="3" tolerance="0" unit="R"}
Your daily loss limit is 3R, and you've lost three trades at −1R each. How many R have you lost today? (After this, your rules say stop.)
? 3 × −1R = −3R, which reaches your 3R daily limit. Your rules end the day here.
::

---

A losing day is a normal part of trading. The aim isn't to avoid them; it's to keep a bad day from becoming a disaster.

::takeaway
Set your loss limits while calm, write them down, and stop when you hit them. A small losing day is normal; a revenge spiral is avoidable.
::
