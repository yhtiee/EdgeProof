Before learning how to trade, it's worth knowing how most people who try it actually do. Not from adverts or social media, but from regulators and researchers who can see real accounts.

---

Europe's securities regulator looked at accounts across many brokers. It found that 74–89% of retail CFD accounts typically lose money on their investments [@esma-cfd-restrictions-2018].

That's why EU and UK brokers have to show a warning with the percentage of their own clients who lose money.

---

Researchers in Brazil studied everyone who started day trading futures over three years. Of the individuals who persisted in day trading for more than 300 days, 97% lost money [@chague-day-trading-2019].

They also found no evidence that day traders learn by trading. Doing it for longer didn't make people better.

---

Trading a lot tends to make things worse, not better. Of 66,465 US brokerage households studied from 1991 to 1996, those that traded most earned 11.4% a year, while the market returned 17.9% [@barber-odean-trading-hazardous].
