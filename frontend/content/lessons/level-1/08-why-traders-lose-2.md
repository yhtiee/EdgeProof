Losing traders aren't stupid. They usually fall into the same few traps. Knowing them by name is the first step to avoiding them.

---

**Trap 1: no tested edge.** Most people trade ideas they've never tested: a pattern from a video, a tip, a feeling. Without testing, there's no way to know whether the idea makes money after costs or just looked good on a few charts.

**Trap 2: risking too much.** With [[leverage]], a small move can cost a big share of the account. Risking 10% per trade means a short losing streak, which happens to everyone, can empty the account.

---

**Trap 3: costs.** The [[spread]], commission and [[swap]] are paid on every trade. Someone who trades often with tight stops can lose to costs even with a decent idea.

**Trap 4: behaviour.** Moving a stop further away, trading to win back a loss, doubling up after a win. These come from fear and excitement, not from a plan.

Most losses come from four traps: no tested edge, too much risk, costs, and emotional decisions. Each one can be checked and limited before real money is at stake.
