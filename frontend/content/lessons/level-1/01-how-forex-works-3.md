Central banks handle monetary policy and manage official reserves for their nations [@fed-monetary-policy]. They adjust official interest rates to manage inflation and balance economic growth.

Unlike profit-focused market participants, central banks trade currencies to stabilize national financial conditions and meet macroeconomic goals.

---

Commercial banks act as the backbone of the interbank market, matching large client orders and managing currency risks on their balance sheets [@babypips-pipsology].

Multinational corporations also execute large transactions. They convert funds to pay overseas suppliers, compensate global workers, and bring foreign earnings back to their domestic accounts.

---

Retail traders participate through an online [[broker]] to speculate on exchange rate changes, using [[leverage]] to control larger positions without ever taking delivery of the currencies.

Individual retail traders are a small fraction of overall trading volume, which is dominated by banks and other large financial institutions.

::takeaway
Most forex trading is done by banks and other large institutions, alongside corporations and central banks. Retail traders are a small share, and most of them trade to speculate on price changes.
