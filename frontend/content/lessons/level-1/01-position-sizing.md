Your position size comes from your stop, not from how sure you feel.

::simulator{kind="position-size"}

When the stop moves further away, the size shrinks. The money at risk stays the same. That is the whole idea.

::question{id="l1-ps-q1"}

::takeaway
Decide the risk first, place the stop where the idea is wrong, then let the size fall out of the maths.
::
